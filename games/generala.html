    How to play

    1. Roll all five dice.
    2. Tap any dice you want to keep, then roll the rest. You get up to three rolls a turn.
    3. Pick one empty box on your card and press Score. If your dice don't fit any box you still need, score a 0 in one of them.
    4. Every box is used once. After ten turns each, the highest total wins.
    1s to 6sTotal of that number

    Servida: made on the first roll

    If you score a Straight or Full house straight from your first roll of the turn, without rerolling, you get 5 extra points (25 and 35). A Generala servida either wins the game instantly or is worth 100, depending on the option chosen when the game started.

    Keyboard and TV remote: 1 to 5 hold dice, R rolls, S scores the chosen box, arrow keys move between buttons.

    Settings

    Show the game on a TV

    1. Android: swipe down from the top, tap Screen cast or Smart View, and pick your TV.
    2. iPhone or iPad: open Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your TV.
    3. Computer with Chrome: open the ⋮ menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab.
    4. Turn on TV mode above, and turn your phone sideways to see the dice and the full score card side by side.
