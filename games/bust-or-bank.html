Game setup

    How to play

    1. Roll all six dice.
    2. Tap at least one scoring die to keep it. Every die you keep must be part of a score.
    3. Roll the rest to add more, or bank your turn total. You need 300 or more in one turn to bank.
    4. If a roll has no scoring dice, you've gone bust: you lose everything from this turn.
    5. If all six dice end up kept, that's hot dice: roll all six again and keep building.

    The first player to reach 10,000 banked points wins straight away.
