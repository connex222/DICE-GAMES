    How to play

    1. Everyone gets ten dice, already rolled.
    2. Choose a number to collect. Tap a die showing it to keep it. Kept dice turn blue and stay put.
    3. You can only keep dice that show the same number. To switch numbers, tap your kept dice to let them go first.
    4. Roll the dice you haven't kept. Keep any new matches and roll again.
    5. The moment all ten dice show the same number, you're done.

    Race

    Everyone plays at the same time. First to ten of a kind wins. Playing alone, you race the clock instead.

    Turn by turn

    Players take one roll each in turn. Whoever finishes in the fewest rolls wins, and everyone gets the same number of rolls before a winner is called. Playing alone, try to beat your fewest-rolls record.

    Keyboard and TV remote: 1 to 9 and 0 keep dice, R or Space rolls, P pauses, arrow keys move between buttons.

    Settings

    Show the game on a TV

    1. Android: swipe down from the top, tap Screen cast or Smart View, and pick your TV.
    2. iPhone or iPad: open Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your TV.
    3. Computer with Chrome: open the ⋮ menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab.
    4. Turn on TV mode above.

    Solo races and turn-by-turn games work well on a TV. A race between several people needs everyone touching the same screen, so play that on a tablet instead.
