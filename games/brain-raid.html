Game setup

You're a zombie. Grab dice from the cup and roll for brains. Footprints mean your victim ran, so they roll again. Too many shotgun hits and you lose the lot. First to the target sets up the final round.

    How to play

    1. Take dice at random from the cup to fill your hand, and roll them. Horde rolls 6 at a time, Classic rolls 3.
    2. 🧠 Brains are points and are set aside for this turn.
    3. 💥 Shotguns hit you. They're set aside too.
    4. 👣 Footprints mean your victim got away. Those dice stay in the tray and roll again.
    5. After each roll, choose: stop and bank your brains, or push your luck and roll again.
    6. Take too many hits (6 in Horde, 3 in Classic) and you're blasted: the turn scores nothing.

    Special faces (Horde)

    🧠×3 Genius brain on purple and wild dice: worth 3 brains.

    💥×2 Double barrel on blue brute dice: counts as 2 hits.

    💣 Bomb, only on the single wild die: instant blast, whatever your hits.

    🧨 Lucky bomb, also only on the wild die: blows away every shotgun you've taken this turn.

    Winning

    When someone banks the target (25 in Horde, 13 in Classic), everyone left in that round still gets a turn. The highest total then wins. A tie is settled by one more round between the tied players.

    Running out of dice

    If the cup can't fill your hand, the brains you've set aside go back in the cup. You keep the points for them. Shotguns stay out.

    Keyboard and TV remote: R rolls, S scores and stops, N continues, arrow keys move between buttons.

    Settings

    Show the game on a TV

    1. Android: swipe down from the top, tap Screen cast or Smart View, and pick your TV.
    2. iPhone or iPad: open Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your TV.
    3. Computer with Chrome: open the ⋮ menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab.
    4. Turn on TV mode above, and turn your phone sideways for the widescreen layout.
