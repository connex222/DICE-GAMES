Liar's Dice

New game

Game setup

Everyone rolls in secret. Bid on how many of a face are hidden under all the cups, and call Liar! when you think someone's bluffing. Last player with dice wins.

Game in progress

Players (2 to 6)

    Bidding with aces

    How you're playing

    1how many

    How to play

    1. Everyone starts with five dice and rolls them in secret under a cup. You can only see your own.
    2. On your turn, bid how many dice of one face you think are under all the cups put together, for example "four 5s".
    3. The next player either raises the bid, or calls Liar! to challenge it.
    4. On a challenge every cup is lifted and the dice are counted.
    5. If there are at least as many as the bid, the challenger loses a die. If there are fewer, the bidder loses a die.
    6. Everyone rerolls and a new round starts with the player who lost. Lose all your dice and you're out.

    The last player with dice wins.

    Aces are wild

    1s count as any face. A bid of "four 5s" counts every 5 and every 1. If someone bids on aces themselves, only 1s count.

    Raising a bid

    Bid a higher quantity, or the same quantity of a higher face. With Perudo-style aces, you can switch to aces with half the quantity (rounded up), and back from aces with double plus one.

    Keyboard and TV remote: 1 to 6 pick a face, + and − change how many, B bids, L calls Liar, N continues, arrow keys move between buttons.

    Settings

    Show the game on a TV

    1. Android: swipe down from the top, tap Screen cast or Smart View, and pick your TV.
    2. iPhone or iPad: open Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your TV.
    3. Computer with Chrome: open the ⋮ menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab.
    4. Turn on TV mode above, and turn your phone sideways for the widescreen layout.

    Your dice show on the TV too. Mirroring copies the whole screen. On a TV, play one person against bots, or turn on Hold to peek so dice only appear while a player holds their cup and everyone else looks away.
