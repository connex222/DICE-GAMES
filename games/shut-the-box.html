Game setup

Roll two dice and flip down tiles that add up to the roll. Keep going until you can't. Whatever's left standing is your score, so lower is better. Flip them all and you've shut the box.

    How you're playing

    Pass and play on one phone, tablet or TV. Bots play their own turns. Online rooms need a game server, which isn't switched on in this offline build.

    Roll –

    How to play

    1. Every turn starts with all the tiles standing.
    2. Roll two dice and add them up.
    3. Flip down any tiles that make exactly that total. A 9 could be the 9, or 5 + 4, or 1 + 2 + 6.
    4. Roll again and repeat.
    5. When no standing tiles can make your roll, your turn ends. Your score is the total of the tiles still standing.

    Lowest score wins. Flip every tile and you've shut the box, for a perfect score of 0.

    Options

    One die: once all tiles from 7 upward are down, you may roll one die instead of two.

    Instant win: with two or more players, shutting the box ends the game on the spot.

    Rounds: with 3 or 5 rounds, scores add up and the lowest total wins.

    Keyboard and TV remote: 1 to 9 pick tiles (0 for 10), R rolls two dice, O rolls one, S shuts, C clears, N continues, arrow keys move between buttons.

    Settings

    Show the game on a TV

    1. Android: swipe down from the top, tap Screen cast or Smart View, and pick your TV.
    2. iPhone or iPad: open Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your TV.
    3. Computer with Chrome: open the ⋮ menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab.
    4. Turn on TV mode above, and turn your phone sideways so all the tiles sit in one row.
